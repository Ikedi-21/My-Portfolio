Engineering with Delivery Discipline

I am a full-stack developer based in Enugu, Nigeria. My focus centers on architecting resilient web applications — from structuring relational databases and API boundaries to deploying production user interfaces.

I view project management not as a separate career path, but as the critical discipline that sets my engineering apart. Defining strict scope bounds, capturing architectural trade-offs in writing, and running transparent sprint cycles ensures software ships cleanly without surprise friction.
